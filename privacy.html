Privacy Policy

App name: yt automation
Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. About this app

yt automation is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Data accessed

With the owner's explicit permission through Google OAuth, the app accesses the owner's own YouTube channel data, including video uploads, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, playlists, comments, and YouTube Analytics reports for that channel.

3. How data is used

This data is used only to upload and edit videos, review channel performance, and manage the owner's channel. It is not used for any other purpose.

4. Data storage and sharing

The app does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data with any third party. Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never transmitted to any server other than Google's.

5. Google API Services disclosure

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
